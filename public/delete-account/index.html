Account Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from the Your Pocket Soul Coach app, you can request data deletion at any time.

How to delete your account from within the app:
  • Open the Your Pocket Soul Coach app.
  • Go to your Profile or Settings page.
  • Tap on the Delete Account option.
  • Confirm the deletion. All your data will be permanently removed immediately.

Alternatively, if you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team via email. Please include the email address associated with your account.
